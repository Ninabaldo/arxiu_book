There are moments in life when you have no idea you're about to begin a whole new chapter. There's no background music, no special light, no voice telling you that what is happening right now is going to change your life. You're simply getting on with your life and, all of a sudden, someone says a sentence to you that you weren't expecting to hear and you're left trying to work out at what point you went from having everything more or less under control to not even knowing what you'll be doing tomorrow.

It happened to me on a Saturday morning.

Marcos looked at me and said:

—I HAVE TO TELL YOU SOMETHING.

Oof.

That's a sentence I'm not sure we're ever quite prepared for. Because, obviously, it depends on the context, but there's an age at which, when someone tells you "I have to tell you something", you already suspect they're not exactly about to announce that they've won the lottery.

And before I officially went into shock, I still had time to call him every name under the sun. Obviously, I didn't understand a thing. After six years together, having a person suddenly tell you something that completely rewrites the script of your life is not easy to take in. My brain was trying to find an explanation while I was still trying to understand what was happening.

And then Marcos left.

He left me there, in his flat, packing suitcases.

Just like that.

I was leaving behind a life that until that moment I had taken for granted.

And then came five months.

Six candidates.

Out.
