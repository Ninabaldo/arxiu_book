From that moment on I carried on analysing every step Max took. When he wrote to me, when he didn't, what he wrote to me, how often… surprisingly, he did all of it with an unselfconsciousness that threw me off a little. But I was calm. I trusted my intuition and, after everything I'd been through, I knew I could listen to it.

What I didn't want, obviously, was to get myself back into a story with someone who wasn't clear about things. With love, Marcos, but get it together.

Not long after, since WhatsApp had already become our main channel of communication, I started opening up a bit more with Max. And here, seen with hindsight, it looked as though the story had ended before it began. Inside, it had been amazing, I'd liked it a lot, but at the same time I had an enormous barrier telling me that I didn't want to continue.

So, naturally, I put him to the test again.

I told him I wasn't ready.

It wasn't exactly true.

But I wanted to see what he'd do with that information.

His answer was:

—I hope your recovery from your breakup goes well.

That stung.

Hahahaha.

I don't know exactly what I was expecting him to say, but that sentence hit my ego. So I ended up telling him the truth: I'd felt too exposed and vulnerable. I'd liked it a lot, but it had also frightened me.

And here Max surprised me.

He didn't defend himself, he didn't pressure me and he didn't pretend nothing was going on. He responded with a lot of empathy, understanding what I was telling him and making me feel more protected. And that allowed me to carry on trying.

So, shortly afterwards, I told him I'd be spending a few days near his place.

He must have been baffled.

Going from telling him I wasn't ready to announcing that I'd be near his place was, to say the least, an interesting plot development.

I was laughing at myself, thinking: ohmygod, how little consistency I'm showing.

But what do you want me to say? I can't control everything.

Luckily, he didn't pay much attention to my contradictions either, and he suggested we see each other.

And so the moment of the second date arrived.

Well.

The supposed second date.

Because we never even got to have it.

I'd booked a hotel and I'd paid for it myself. I suppose hotels felt familiar to me and, at that moment, it must have seemed like a perfectly normal idea. The night before, however, a virus decided to intervene in our story and said to me:

"Sweetheart, tonight you're staying in bed".

And I got ill.

With a fever.

So I had to write to Max to tell him I was wrecked and that we probably wouldn't be able to see each other.

I imagine he must already have been thinking that this girl was a bit complicated. And I don't blame him.

Because, from the outside, the sequence was pretty funny: first "I'm not ready", then "I'll be near your place", then I book a hotel and, just when it looks like we'll finally see each other, I get ill.

What patience, Max.

The curious thing is that he didn't say much. He didn't make scenes, he didn't ask me for explanations. The only thing I started to notice was that there was a bit more space between our conversations.

And that was normal.

I would have done the same.

But I noticed it.

And, while my fever climbed and I had a hotel already paid for, I still tried to see whether there was some way I could meet up with him. I dragged myself as best I could to the hotel, because, obviously, if I'd booked a room, my mission was to make the most of it right to the end.

I arrived, I had a nap, I showered and I took a good dose of medication with the intention of resurrecting myself.

It didn't work.

After that whole logistical deployment, I had to accept that I couldn't see him and I told him we'd leave it for another time.

Very kindly, he answered that health was health and that he would have liked to have sex with me, but that it would be another day.

And I laughed.

But I also thought:

Excuse me?

Because, at that moment, my ego came out.

I wanted him to come and see me, to talk to me, to spend a while together. I didn't want him assuming that the only reason I'd booked a hotel was to have sex with him again.

And here I thought that maybe I'd contributed a little to the confusion too.

Because, let's be honest: at what point does a girl book a hotel, get ill, cross half the world with a fever and expect a man to understand that actually she just wanted to chat?

I know.

Once again, not much consistency.

I don't want to be too hard on myself, because I also recognise that there were things I couldn't help at that moment. I was trying to protect myself, to understand what I was feeling and, at the same time, to keep letting myself go. And those two things didn't always point in the same direction.

So that second date ended the way it had begun: without ever happening.

I ended up sleeping alone in the hotel, with the Frenadol as the night's only guest.

And Max, who by this stage was probably trying to work out exactly what kind of woman he'd gone and met, had something I still didn't know how to value enough:

patience.
