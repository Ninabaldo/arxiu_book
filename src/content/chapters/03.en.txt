After all that, obviously, I wasn't about to apologise for being ill. But if there was one thing I was starting to understand about myself, it was that I have two fairly recognisable sides. One is the side that protects itself from everything: it puts up a wall, says "this is where I draw the line" and, all of a sudden, only sees what the other person is doing wrong —or what they're doing because they don't really know what to do— and decides to smash it all up before anyone can hurt her. The other is capable of standing beside the other person, understanding where they're coming from and owning her own mistakes too. I suppose that's what growing up is. I suppose.

Over the following days, Max was kind. He asked how I was doing, he checked in on me, but I won't lie to you: I didn't exactly see him as especially invested either. And I understood. After all, I'd just done a bit of everything: first yes, then no, then a hotel, then a fever, then better another day… What patience. But he was still there. Max is pretty steady and he comes across as serious, and I kept wondering what must be going through his head. Our conversations weren't as intense as they'd been those first few days, but I was still interested. I simply let the hours pass and watched.

It had all happened on a Tuesday night. By Wednesday night I was back home, and on Friday I decided to take a more grown-up step: to tell him clearly what I was thinking. I admitted that I would have liked to have had that date, that I was feeling better and that I was grateful he'd worried about me. And, above all, I told him that I wanted to keep seeing him. If he didn't, no problem. Obviously, I would have had to deal with it, but in that moment I said it with spectacular confidence. As if I were absolutely clear about what I wanted, what I felt and where all of this was heading. A speciality of mine.

He answered yes very quickly.

And it was really lovely to hear. Because, honestly, I no longer knew what to think.

Not long after, he suggested I come and stay at his place for a few days. He'd be working, but I could settle in there. Me, obviously, over the moon. We never really nailed down the details, but in my head it was already crystal clear.

Until Sunday morning, when my period arrived.

We'd arranged to meet on Tuesday and the maths, honestly, didn't add up at all. Because having a cold is one thing and this is quite another. Come on. Still dragging the cold around, and now my period… What next? A bout of gastroenteritis to complete the set?

I decided to tell him. I didn't want to get my fingers caught, and besides, a man who's been in a relationship shouldn't normally experience these things as if they were the apocalypse, but I had no idea how he'd take it either. He didn't react much. He told me it was up to me.

And I decided we'd meet anyway.

On Tuesday, Max's original plan was to go and look at sofas, because he was in the middle of furnishing his flat, then have a drink in town and dinner at his place. And honestly, I loved the sofa plan. Anything to do with houses, furniture, decorating and imagining how a space could look fascinates me. For me, going to look at sofas was hardly torture. In fact, it seemed like a pretty great plan.

But for a second date, after everything that had happened and, above all, after everything already going on in my head, I wasn't sure about it at all. So I changed the plan on him and suggested we stay in.

I think he felt calmer about it too. But now, looking back with some perspective, I think maybe his original plan made more sense than I was willing to give it credit for. Maybe he was simply trying to break the ice. To do something normal. So that this time it wouldn't look like we were heading straight back to the same square as that first night.

I, on the other hand, had something else in mind. I wanted to really connect with him. To get to know him beyond the attraction, the tension and all that intensity that had shown up so fast. And for that, I didn't want any unnecessary outside distractions.

I wanted to see what happened when it was simply him and me.
